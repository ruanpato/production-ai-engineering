# Part 6 — Costs, Performance and FinOps

> **Thesis of the part.** Cost in AI is not just the price of the model call. The total cost includes infrastructure, observability, evals, retraining, retries, human review and incidents. Without explicit FinOps, agents can burn a monthly budget in hours.
