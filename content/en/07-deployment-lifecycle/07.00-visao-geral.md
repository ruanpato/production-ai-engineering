# Part 7 — Deployment, Lifecycle and Deprecation

> **Thesis of the part.** Agents, models, prompts, tools, MCP servers, schemas, embeddings and indexes have a **lifecycle**. Treating AI as software without versioning, without rollback and without a deprecation plan is the most predictable way to generate incidents.
