# Part 0 — Foundations of AI Systems

> **Thesis of the part.** Before discussing LLMs, RAG, agents, MCP or A2A, we need a shared foundation about what we are building. This part establishes the vocabulary that appears throughout the rest of the book:
> **model vs product vs system**, **probabilistic vs deterministic**, **AI as a sociotechnical system**, **demo vs production**, **control vs mitigation**, and the **architectural decision matrix** that guides the choices in the following parts. It closes by introducing the **"Hermes Logística" case study**, which reappears throughout the book as grounding.
