# Part 4 — Security, Governance and Compliance

> **Thesis of the part.** Security in AI is not a layer you add at the end. It is a set of **controls and mitigations** distributed across data, model, context, tools, memory, runtime, authorization, observability and lifecycle. When a technique does not guarantee 100% protection, this book says so **explicitly**.
