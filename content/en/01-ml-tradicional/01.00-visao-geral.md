# Part 1 — Traditional ML in Production

> **Thesis of the part.** Traditional ML has not gone away. In modern architectures with LLMs and agents, classifiers, rerankers, anomaly detectors, embeddings and routers remain the backbone of what works cheaply, quickly and auditably.
